# AYA RAOS — Brand Journey & Cultural Depth v1.5.1

## Status

Preview refinement. Not a Production Launch authorization.

## Purpose

v1.5.1 refines the approved v1.3–v1.5 foundation without redesigning it. The goal is to make AYA RAOS easier to **understand, feel, respond to, and act on** while keeping the website knowledge-and-culture first and commerce second.

## Homepage journey

```text
AYA RAOS / Ada Rasa
→ Makna RAOS
→ Cerita & budaya: rasa · bahan · meja
→ Kenapa tiga lini
→ Tiga lini AYA + karakter masing-masing
→ Response
→ Hero product / proof
→ Action
```

## Design rules

- Master-brand first viewport stays Heritage Maroon + Cream + Gold.
- Three line colors appear only after master-brand awareness.
- Body readability target is 16px; critical labels/buttons/forms/footer must not rely on tiny type.
- Section rhythm alternates quiet editorial, visual narrative, structure, ecosystem, proof, and conversion. Avoid repeated card-deck treatment.
- Culture is expressed through meaning, food context, product knowledge, ways of enjoying, and approved human/process evidence—not through folklore overload.
- AYA Farm remains honest about actual status and available products; do not fabricate depth to balance the ecosystem visually.

## Evidence boundary

Do not publish or imply founder history, ancestral recipes, ingredient origin, production capacity, client names, certifications, or other cultural/commercial proof without verification and approval.

## Product knowledge

Product Detail labels the existing `suitableUse` field as **Cara Menikmati**. Composition, storage, flavor, and other product information remain evidence-bound.

## Shareability

Key staging pages receive OpenGraph title, description, and image metadata for better link previews. Staging remains `noindex`; canonical, structured data, sitemap/indexing, and Production SEO Launch remain outside this scope.

## Protected integrations

No functional changes to testimonial submission, Supabase client, order API, cart persistence, or business-inquiry persistence.
