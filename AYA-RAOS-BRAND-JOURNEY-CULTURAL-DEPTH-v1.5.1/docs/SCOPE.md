# Scope — AYA RAOS v1.5.1

## In scope

1. Brand journey refinement without redesign.
2. Cultural depth through meaning, food context, knowledge, and ways of enjoying.
3. Readability and hierarchy improvements in the active design system.
4. Homepage narrative pacing.
5. Three-line ecosystem presentation.
6. Product Detail knowledge label (`Cara Menikmati`).
7. Social share OpenGraph metadata.
8. Naming consistency and governance documentation.

## Out of scope

- New product facts or unverified cultural/source stories.
- New product photography presented as real evidence.
- Payment, shipping-rate integration, inventory, account, quotation, analytics.
- Public wholesale pricing or production capacity.
- Production SEO/indexing/canonical activation.
- Protected testimonial/Supabase/order/B2B integration changes.
- Database changes.

## Acceptance principle

The design is accepted only when browser QA confirms that the site feels less like a presentation deck and more like a coherent premium editorial brand journey while retaining clear conversion paths.
