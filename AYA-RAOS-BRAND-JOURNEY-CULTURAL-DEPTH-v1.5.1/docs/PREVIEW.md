# Preview Guidance

`preview/homepage-journey-map-v151.png` is a **static composition map**, not a browser screenshot and not final visual acceptance.

The real preview gate is a Vercel Preview Deployment from a `preview/*` branch.

## What to validate visually

### Homepage

1. AYA RAOS / Ada Rasa is the first and dominant impression.
2. The visitor understands RAOS before being introduced to the three lines.
3. The cultural chapter feels human/editorial, not like an internal brand guideline.
4. The three lines are introduced as one ecosystem but retain their individual worlds.
5. Commercial response appears only after understanding/feeling.
6. Sambal AYA reads as the current hero product, not the definition of AYA RAOS.
7. Testimonial and final actions have enough visual weight.

### Readability

Check small labels, footer, product cards, product detail, forms, B2B, and mobile. No critical information should rely on 8–10px text.

### Responsive

- 1366×768: composition and pacing.
- 1440×900: whitespace and editorial rhythm.
- 1024×768: line/card collapse and navigation.
- 390×844: mobile conversion, readability, normal scroll.
